About

I'm a data warehouse consultant at Komtaş, working on enterprise data platforms in banking: dimensional models and datamarts, slowly-changing-dimension history, T-SQL stored procedures and SSIS packages, and the code-review, parallel-run and go-live discipline that keeps them trustworthy in production.

Before consulting I trained as an AI & machine-learning engineer. My Koç University capstone, GymBud, won Best Project for combining real-time computer vision with custom wearables, and my internships took ML into production at Odeabank and data engineering at Yapı Kredi Teknoloji. I still approach warehouse work as both a data problem and a systems problem.

I also write field reports about what actually happens when AI coding agents meet production data work: what helped, what broke, and what I would measure next time.
